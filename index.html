Professional Background

I am currently a Senior Lecturer at the Institute of Entrepreneurship at IMC Krems University of Applied Sciences. My career spans academic research, consulting, and market research, with positions at institutions including Technische Universität Berlin, Copenhagen Business School, and University of Vienna. I have also worked as a Senior Researcher at Spectra Marktforschung and as a consultant at TD Reply in Berlin, where I helped international clients discover and evaluate future opportunities through data-driven marketing and innovation.

Education

I hold a PhD in Management from the University of Vienna (2015), where my dissertation focused on business model innovation. I also earned an M.Sc. in Media Design from the University of Applied Arts Vienna (2010) and an M.Sc. in International Business Administration from the University of Vienna (2007).
